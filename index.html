OKIWWW THE LORE

Ni Hao Bubble tea is generally traced back to Taiwan in the 1980s. The exact origin story? A bit of a debate actually. FAHHH !!

BEFORE THE BOBA

Tea was already everywhere.

Taiwan already had a big tea culture. Cold tea, sweet drinks different toppings none of this came out of nowhere. The magic was in putting the pieces together. SKABOOSH BABOOSH !!

THE 1980s

THE GREAT PEARL INCIDENT.

One famous story credits Chun Shui Tang(What an odd name) in Taichung. The story goes that tapioca balls were added to iced tea and people liked it alot.

Another shop also claims a part in the invention, so the origin isn't completely settled. History is annoying like that.(I hate history lowkey)
